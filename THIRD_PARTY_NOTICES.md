# Third-party components and services

MusicPlayer loads these components externally; they are not bundled here:

- [Butterchurn 2.6.7](https://github.com/jberg/butterchurn): MIT license.
  [Upstream license](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/butterchurn@2.6.7/LICENSE).
- [Butterchurn Presets 2.4.7](https://github.com/jberg/butterchurn-presets):
  MIT license according to the published package metadata.
  [Upstream package](https://www.npmjs.com/package/butterchurn-presets/v/2.4.7).
  Preset names retain their creators' credits.
- [Inter](https://github.com/rsms/inter) and
  [Space Grotesk](https://github.com/floriankarsten/space-grotesk), served by
  Google Fonts: SIL Open Font License 1.1. See each project's license.

Radio discovery uses the public [Radio Browser](https://www.radio-browser.info/)
API. Streams and station artwork are provided by their respective stations.

MusicPlayer is adapted from VideoPlayer; its original copyright notice is
retained in LICENSE. Music and album artwork shown in the README screenshot
belong to their respective owners and are not covered by the software's MIT
license. No audio files are distributed with this repository.
